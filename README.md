# Lab research meeting — presentation sign-ups

Two separate sign-ups, one presenter slot per meeting:

- **[METHODS.md](METHODS.md)** — Methods research meeting (Fridays 11–12)
- **[APPLIED.md](APPLIED.md)** — Applied research meeting (Tuesdays 2–3)

Claim a slot on the sign-up page at **https://brucedesmarais.com/labsignup/** (no account needed; pick your meeting and date, enter your name, submit). Claims queue with server timestamps and a scheduled Action applies them to the sheets in arrival order, first come first served. The GitHub issue forms remain as an alternative for account holders. The sheets are public and require no login to view.

## Manual edits

The sheets are plain Markdown tables. A cell containing exactly `OPEN` is claimable; any other text counts as taken. To assign someone by hand, replace `OPEN` with their name; to clear, replace it with `OPEN`. Keep the `| MM/DD | ... |` row format intact — the bot only fills the single claimed cell, so manual edits are preserved.
